1. How clients can pay

Venmo's rules require a business profile for getting paid for work (1.9% + 10¢). Personal accounts can be frozen.

Paid automatically, plans go out within minutes. Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢ per card payment (bank transfer 0.8%, max $5). Get your secret key from dashboard.stripe.com/apikeys; use a test key (sk_test_…) to try it out.

With Zelle and Venmo the app can't see the payment itself. When it arrives in your bank or Venmo app, open Transactions → Mark as paid and the final PDF is emailed straight away. Only mark it paid once you see the money, not from a screenshot.
